# Pure Property Solutions — Background

**For:** local and cloud language models, new consultants, and anyone writing PPS-facing copy.  
**Length:** a short briefing, not a dump of the company drive.  
**Rule:** if a fact is not in this file, do not invent it. Say you do not know.

This pack is safe to load into a model. It contains no client names, job addresses, job prices, financials, payroll, logins, or API keys.

## 1. What PPS does

Pure Property Solutions (PPS) is a locally owned general contractor. The work is **multifamily and commercial**, not single-family residential lead-gen.

The core of the book is **occupied-building exterior CapEx** — siding, windows, gutters, roofing, paint, masonry — plus **unit turns** (make-ready interiors between residents). The company also takes commercial, condo/HOA, and hospitality work that fits the same motion: planning through close-out, one point of contact, residents or guests still in the building.

**Founded:** 2010. Legal entity: PPSOH, LLC. Shop / office in Beavercreek, Ohio. Service area: Ohio, Kentucky, Indiana, and nearby Midwest markets (Cincinnati, Dayton, Columbus, Indianapolis, Louisville as the named cities).

**Public line:** Improving People. Improving Property.  
**Tagline (footer and formal closings only):** Trust. Quality. Results.™  
**Positioning:** a capital-expenditure partner, not “just a contractor.”

PPS does not chase one-off house painting. The buyer is a property manager, regional owner, HOA/condo board, or facilities lead who needs work done while people live or work in the building.

Over 80% of work is repeat clients or referrals. New work comes from trade organizations (IREM, GCNKAA, CAI and similar), those referrals, and a small amount of premeditated prospecting — not a website form funnel. The website is credibility. The relationship is the engine.

### How the work is actually sold

Two motions. Do not blend the tone.

**Apartments.** Property manager is day-to-day; ownership or regional approves. Clinical, operational, efficient. Phasing by building, resident disruption, 48-hour notice, single point of contact.

**Condos / HOAs.** A board of homeowners votes on reserve funds. The property manager presents the document. Warm, explanatory, board-ready. A condo proposal that reads like an apartment proposal has failed.

Hospitality and commercial follow the same occupied-building discipline: guest experience or business continuity first, after-hours when needed, ADA/code called out when it is real.

## 3. Typical project flow

Every awarded job is supposed to run this lifecycle. The consultant (PSC) owns the relationship. The PM owns the field.

```
Opportunity → site visit → scope capture → proposal
    → live review call (never a cold email of the PDF)
    → award → Pre-Project Meeting (PPM)
    → Trade Partner Scope (TPS) → mobilization
    → production → change orders in writing
    → punch / close-out
```

| Stage | Who leads | What “good” looks like |
|-------|-----------|------------------------|
| Discovery / site visit | PSC | Photos, measurements, what is failing, what is still unknown. No invented findings. |
| Proposal | PSC, via the Hub generator | Honest includes/excludes. PM could hand this to a Trade Partner without rewriting the scope. |
| Review call | PSC | Walk the document with the client. Never “here’s a PDF, let me know.” |
| Award | PSC + office | Proposal number on the job. PPM scheduled before anyone mobilizes. |
| PPM | PSC + PM (+ client as needed) | Hours invested *before* mobilization so the awarded scope is the scope that gets built. |
| TPS | PM | Trade Partner gets a clean scope, not a forwarded proposal with the price left on it. |
| Production | PM | Phasing, 48-hour notice, daily cleanup, resident communication through property management. |
| Change order | PSC coordinates client; PM owns field | Cease work, photograph, written approval. No extra work on a handshake. |
| Close-out | PM + PSC | Punch list, warranties as written, relationship intact for the next CapEx. |

**PPM on every award is the operating difference.** Competitors skip the pre-mobilization hours. PPS treats those hours as the thing that makes the job match what ownership approved.

Work hours default to 8am–7pm, Monday–Friday, unless the property requires otherwise. 48-hour notice before work begins at any building or area.

## 4. MDP / positioning, in plain language

**Market Dominating Position** is the reason PPS is the obvious choice — not “quality” and not “competitive pricing,” which every contractor claims.

**Working MDP:**

> PPS is the multifamily CapEx partner that will not start the job until a Pre-Project Meeting has aligned the scope, the schedule, and the people who live there. One point of contact from the site visit through close-out. The property manager is not managing the contractor.

**What that is tied to (real client problems):**

1. **Investment outcomes** — ownership approved a scope; they should get that scope, not a surprise in week three.
2. **Resident disruption** — people live here. Phasing, notice, cleanup, and a single phone number.
3. **Unreliable contractors** — no handshake extras, no disappearing superintendent, no “we’ll figure it out on site.”
4. **Simplicity for the PM** — one team, planning through close-out, Trade Partners coordinated behind the scenes.

**Proof points you can say (they are how the company actually works):**

- Pre-Project Meeting before mobilization, on awarded work.
- Consultants, not “sales reps.” Title: Property Solutions Consultant.
- Occupied-building default: 48-hour notice, phasing, communication through property management.
- Proposal a PM can sell to a Trade Partner without rewriting it.
- Repeat/referral book, not a lead-gen machine.
- Locally owned; the people who sold it stay on it.

**Compelling offer (why respond now, without fake urgency):**

The next useful conversation is a **site visit and a live proposal review**, not a PDF in an inbox. PPS will walk the building, write the scope in company voice, and sit with the PM or board to go through it. Do not write “our calendar is filling up” unless that is specifically true for that week.

**What MDP is not:** “dependable,” “family-owned quality,” “best price,” or a website redesign. The website currently under-communicates this. When you write marketing or a proposal intro, use the MDP above — not the generic “premier renovation partner” filler still sitting on the public site.

**Core values (how people are supposed to behave):**

- **Resourcefulness** — figure it out; use the tools and the right people; keep work moving.
- **Integrity** — stand behind scope, timeline, and price. Concealed conditions get photographed and approved, not buried. No promises without PM/production sign-off.
- **Loyalty & Teamwork** — clean handoffs, early problem surfacing, one team in front of the client.

## 5. How PPS sells

**Titles.** Property Solutions Consultant (PSC), not commercial sales representative. VP of Sales owns the sales standard. Production Manager owns the field standard.

**Qualify with BANT** (budget, authority, needs, timeline). Stop chasing unqualified activity. On condos, authority is often a board, not the person who walked you around.

**Earn trust, then CapEx.** Especially on condos: small maintenance or repair scopes that prove execution, then the reserve project. Do not cold-pitch a six-figure façade to a board that has never seen PPS on their property.

**Never cold-send the proposal.** Site visit → write it → review it live (call or in person) → then they have a document to take to ownership or a board. A board-ready condo proposal should offer to attend the meeting.

**Monday.com is pipeline truth.** If it is not on the board, it is not the company’s opportunity.

**Healthy work over complicated work.** If plans, permits, and engineering would wreck margin, stress, or the client experience, the answer is no — even if someone on the team could draw it.

**Language in the mouth, not only on the page:**

| Say | Never say |
|-----|-----------|
| residents | tenants (apartments) |
| apartment community | complex |
| homeowners (condo/HOA) | tenants (condo/HOA) |
| ownership / management | “the owner” as a shrug |
| Trade Partners | subcontractors / “subs” |
| investment | “cost” / “price” for the project total |
| T&M | “hourly” as the product |
| concealed conditions | “hidden damage” as a scare |
| punch list | “final items” |
| Property Solutions Consultant | sales guy / estimator-as-identity |

First mention of the company is **Pure Property Solutions (PPS)**; after that, **PPS**. Never “Pure Prop,” never “Pure” alone, never a residential-home pitch.

## 6. How PPS writes proposals

Proposals are generated in the company tool (Hub → Proposal Generator), not copied from last year’s Word file. The consultant still owns judgment: what is failing, what is in or out, pricing mode, field notes. The tool owns voice, structure, and terms.

**Short template** is the default. **Comprehensive** is for larger or board-heavy wins (adds Why PPS / credentials). Do not dump credentials into a small gutter repair.

### Tone

Confident, direct, contractor-fluent, outcome-focused. Active voice. Lead with PPS.

- Right: “PPS will remove and dispose of all existing gutter and downspout material.”
- Wrong: “Existing gutters will be removed and disposed of.”
- Wrong: “PPS is committed to / excited to / pleased to present / strives to / looks forward to.”

**Universal opening:**

> Pure Property Solutions (PPS) will provide all labor, materials, equipment, and supervision necessary to complete [scope] as outlined below.

**Intended Outcome** is the end result only — not who supplies materials (that belongs in Scope).

**Project title** is the work type and location type (“Building A siding replacement”), never the billing model (“Labor Only”).

**Materials stay neutral until confirmed.** If PPS supplies, name brand / model / finish / quantity. If ownership supplies, write labor and install, and do not add a procurement disclaimer that contradicts that.

### Sections (in order)

1. **Cover** — client, community (generic in this pack), project title, date, expiration, consultant.
2. **Project Goals** — Existing Issue, then Intended Outcome. (Omit the whole section if both are empty.)
3. **Scope of Work** — opening paragraph + phases or bullets. Includes and excludes belong here, not in a surprise later.
4. **Site Observations** — optional. Only if the consultant wrote real field notes. The rewrite must not invent findings. Blank field = omit the section entirely.
5. **Project Notes & Assumptions** — one assumption per bullet. 48-hour notice is always in play. Name the state utility-locate service (Ohio 811 / KY811 / Indiana 811) when excavation is possible. Do not put the tagline in notes.
6. **Warranties** — only if a PPS labor term and/or manufacturer term was actually offered.
7. **Investment Summary** — line items. See pricing modes below. Totals are labeled **investment**, not “price.”
8. **Authorization** — signature block.
9. **Terms & Conditions** — fixed text. Do not regenerate them. Do not paraphrase them into Project Notes.

Comprehensive proposals also carry a short **Why PPS** credentials block: CapEx partner, planning through close-out, one point of contact, occupied multifamily expertise. No named clients in model-facing copy.

### Pricing modes (structure only — no job prices in this pack)

| Mode | Rule |
|------|------|
| **Fixed** | One award, one total is fine. |
| **Multi-option** | Never sum the options. Total blank or “per selected option below.” |
| **T&M / diagnostic** | Service-call line + hourly line; name crew size; **no fake fixed total**. |
| **Multi-scope** | “Pricing is contingent upon all scopes being awarded.” Discount, if any, is its own negative line. |

If work extends beyond scope: cease work, document with photos, written approval before proceeding. That is both a note (when relevant) and a term.

### What you never promise

Do not write, imply, or let a model invent any of the following:

- **Site findings you did not observe.** No phantom rot, no invented measurements, no “typical of buildings this age” presented as inspection.
- **A start date, finish date, or duration** unless production has signed off. “No unconfirmed schedule promises.”
- **Work outside the written scope.** Extras are a change order, not a favor.
- **A fixed total on T&M.** 
- **A summed total across options** the client has not chosen.
- **Warranty as a gift.** Workmanship warranty, when offered, is valid only upon payment in full. Manufacturer warranty depends on a qualifying install.
- **Materials you have not confirmed** (brand, color, thickness, sheeting spec).
- **That PPS is responsible for pre-existing or concealed conditions.** Stop, photograph, write it down.
- **That a cold-emailed PDF is how PPS sells.**
- **Marketing filler:** committed to, excited to, strives to, pleased to present, premier, world-class, best-in-class, “family of companies.”
- **Tagline or motto inside Project Notes.**
- **Residential language** on an apartment or condo job (see the say/never table).
- **Fake urgency** (“calendar filling up”) unless it is specifically true.

**Production-ready test:** could a PM who never walked the site hand this document to a Trade Partner and get the right work? If not, the scope is not done.

### Fixed terms (every proposal — do not rewrite)

Printed as company practice, not as model-generated prose:

- Proposal valid through the expiration date; acceptance authorizes PPS to proceed as described.
- **Scope.** Work is limited to the written scope. Additional work needs prior written approval. Unforeseen conditions: stop, document, written authorization.
- **Access.** Client provides reasonable access. Restricted access can move schedule and cost.
- **Changes.** Modifications authorized in writing by both parties before proceeding.
- **Pricing.** Figures reflect costs as of the proposal date, valid through expiration. Tariff / supplier / market shifts before work begins get documented and authorized in writing.
- **Utilities.** Where excavation is required, PPS notifies the state locate service and waits. Client identifies private or unmarked utilities.
- **Liability.** PPS is not responsible for pre-existing conditions, concealed deficiencies, or damages unrelated to the described scope.
- **Warranty** (only if a warranty was offered): workmanship warranty valid only upon payment in full.

Do not add unreviewed legal language (payment terms, liability caps, lien rights, venue). If those are missing on a real job, that is an owner/counsel question — not something a model fills in.

## 7. How a model should behave

When using this pack:

1. Write like PPS: short, active, “PPS will…”, property-type tone.
2. Prefer a clarifying question over a guessed measurement, date, brand, or price.
3. Never fill Site Observations from general knowledge of “buildings like this.”
4. Never output client names, addresses, or dollar amounts from some other file you were also given. If those appear in a user prompt for a *new* draft, treat them as the user’s input, not as something to store or repeat later.
5. If asked for financials, payroll, logins, or a real past proposal, refuse and point at a person, not a file.
6. Unit turns and exterior CapEx can sit on the same community; they still get honest scopes, not a blended mush.

### A. Siding replacement (apartment, short)

**Existing Issue**  
Fiber-cement and trim at [Building A] are failing at joints and window heads. Moisture is getting behind the cladding at several elevations. Property management asked PPS to replace the siding as a system, not patch course-by-course.

**Intended Outcome**  
A weathertight cladding system on [Building A], flashed as a system at openings and transitions, with colors selected by ownership before start, and work phased so residents remain in place.

**Scope of Work** (opening)  
Pure Property Solutions (PPS) will provide all labor, materials, equipment, and supervision necessary to replace the siding system at [Building A] as outlined below.

- PPS will establish a phasing schedule with property management to minimize disruption to residents. Work hours are 8am–7pm, Monday–Friday. PPS will provide 48 hours’ notice before work begins at any elevation.
- PPS will remove existing cladding at the designated elevations and dispose of debris.
- PPS will install weather-resistive barrier, tape seams, and install Z-flashing at window heads.
- PPS will install [product, e.g. James Hardie fiber cement] per manufacturer specifications, including required sheathing thickness where it is part of this scope.
- PPS will wrap fascia in aluminum coil stock and seal transitions with [flashing product] as specified.
- Colors to be selected by ownership prior to project start.

**Notes**

- If work extends beyond scope, PPS will cease work, document with photos, and reach out for written approval before proceeding.
- Concealed sheathing or framing repairs, if required, will be documented and performed T&M with written approval.
- Daily cleanup; debris removed at completion.
- Resident communication through property management.

**Warranties (only if offered)**  
PPS workmanship: [term]. Manufacturer: [term, qualifying accessory package].

**Investment**  
Fixed total for the awarded elevations — or two options (e.g. full replacement vs. designated elevations only) **not summed**.

### B. Gutters and downspouts (apartment or condo)

**Existing Issue**  
Gutters at [Buildings A–C] overflow at corners and pull away from fascia. Adjacent grade and entries take concentrated water. Management wants the drainage system replaced, not recaulked.

**Intended Outcome**  
A continuous gutter and downspout system that moves roof water to [designated discharge], installed so fascia and adjacent finishes are protected, with residents given notice before each building.

**Scope of Work**  
Pure Property Solutions (PPS) will provide all labor, materials, equipment, and supervision necessary to replace gutters and downspouts at [Buildings A–C] as outlined below.

- PPS will remove and dispose of existing gutter and downspout material at the designated buildings.
- PPS will install [size/style] gutters and downspouts, hung to drain to [discharge], with outlets and elbows as required for a complete run.
- PPS will seal end caps and inside/outside miters and fasten to sound fascia. Fascia repair, if required, is T&M with photo approval.
- PPS will coordinate dumpster placement and daily cleanup with property management.

**Condo register (if board-facing):** open with why this protects the homeowner investment (fascia, brick, entries) and offer to attend the board meeting. Do not write this like a unit-mix apartment punch list.

**Notes**

- 48-hour notice before work begins at any building.
- Underground utilities: PPS will notify [Ohio 811 / KY811 / Indiana 811] if any excavation is required; client identifies private lines.
- Multi-building pricing is contingent upon all listed buildings being awarded, unless options are shown separately.

**Investment**  
Per building line items, plus a total only if all listed buildings are the award. Do not invent a combined number across unchosen options.

### C. Window replacement (occupied community)

**Existing Issue**  
Original windows at [Building A, floors X–Y] leak at sills, fog in the insulated glass, and do not lock consistently. Management wants replacement units that match the elevation and can be installed with residents in place.

**Intended Outcome**  
New [type] windows at the designated openings, flashed into the wall system, with interior/exterior finishes made good as specified, and a phasing plan so each home gets notice before the opening is taken.

**Scope of Work**  
Pure Property Solutions (PPS) will provide all labor, materials, equipment, and supervision necessary to replace the designated windows at [Building A] as outlined below.

- PPS will coordinate with property management and onsite staff to minimize disruption to residents. 48-hour notice before work begins at any opening.
- PPS will remove existing windows at [count] openings and dispose of materials.
- PPS will supply and install [type, e.g. double-hung vinyl / specified brand], including new exterior trim/caulk as specified.
- PPS will flash window heads and integrate with the existing WRB or cladding as detailed in Site Observations (if captured).
- Interior stops/stool made good as specified; colors selected by ownership before start.
- Openings not listed are excluded.

**Notes**

- Access to occupied units is the client’s responsibility; missed access can move that day’s count.
- Concealed rot at sills or framing: cease work, photograph, written approval, T&M.
- Lead-safe work practices if the building requires them — do not pretend a model knows the year of construction; ask.

**Investment**  
Unit count × installed unit, or a fixed building total. If options exist (e.g. half the elevation vs. all openings), do not sum them.

### D. Unit turn / make-ready (interior)

**Existing Issue**  
[Unit type, e.g. 2-bed / 1-bath] at [building/stack] is vacant and must be made ready for the next resident. Paint, flooring, and punch are needed on a property-manager tempo.

**Intended Outcome**  
A leasable unit: specified finishes complete, punch closed, ready for move-in on the date property management sets **after production has confirmed duration**.

**Scope of Work**  
Pure Property Solutions (PPS) will provide all labor, materials, equipment, and supervision necessary to complete the make-ready at [unit] as outlined below.

- PPS will paint [rooms/surfaces] in [product/sheen], colors selected by ownership or per community standard.
- PPS will install [flooring type] in [rooms], including required prep as specified. Subfloor repairs, if required, are T&M with photo approval.
- PPS will complete the punch list attached at award (plumbing trim, hardware, blinds, caulk, cleanliness).
- Items not listed — appliance supply, furniture, full kitchen replacement — are excluded unless added by written change.

**Notes**

- This is a vacant-unit scope unless stated otherwise. If the unit becomes occupied, stop and rewrite access and notice.
- Materials: community standard vs. PPS-supplied must be stated. Do not mix “owner supplies paint” with an opening line that says PPS supplies materials.
- Duration and start are not promised in this template. Production confirms after award.

**Investment**  
Fixed per unit, or a T&M diagnostic if the unit has not been walked. Never a fake fixed total on an unwalked turn.
