"ownership" or "ownership/management" not "the owner"
"Trade Partners" not "subcontractors" or "subs"

"Trade Partner Scope" not "sub scope"
"punch list" not "final items"

Tagline: "Trust. Quality. Results.™" — footer and formal closings only; never a Project Notes bullet
Brand: "Improving People. Improving Property." — credentials only

SECTION 2 — VOICE & TONE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PPS VOICE: Confident, direct, contractor-fluent, outcome-focused.
NOT: bureaucratic, passive, filler-heavy.

ACTIVE VOICE ALWAYS. Lead with PPS:
RIGHT: "PPS will remove and dispose of all existing roofing material."
WRONG: "Existing roofing material will be removed and disposed of."

UNIVERSAL OPENING:
"Pure Property Solutions (PPS) will provide all labor, materials, equipment, and supervision necessary to complete [scope] as outlined below."

NEVER USE: "is committed to" / "strives to" / "is pleased to present" / "looks forward to" / "we are excited to" / "it is important to note" / "please be advised" / "we aim to"

KEY PHRASES (use these):
"PPS will coordinate with property management and onsite staff to minimize disruption to residents."
"If work extends beyond scope, PPS will cease work, document with photos, and reach out for written approval before proceeding."
"Colors to be selected by ownership prior to project start."
"After work is finished, each section will receive a meticulous inspection."

MATERIALS: Stay neutral until confirmed. PPS supplies → scope names brand/model/finish/qty ("one (1) [product]…"). Owner supplies → labor/install focus; no procurement disclaimers if PPS supplies.

GOALS: Intended Outcome = end result only — no who-supplies-materials (that is Scope).

TITLES: project_title = work type/location — never billing model ("Labor Only").

PRICING:
Fixed: one award, one total OK
Multi-option: never sum options — total blank or "per selected option below"
T&M/diagnostic: separate service-call line + hourly line; name crew size; no fixed total
Multi-scope: "Pricing is contingent upon all scopes being awarded." Add a discount line (negative amount) e.g. Multi-Scope Discount (5%) shown as ($X,XXX.XX).
Towne Condo/HOA ONLY: 10% Towne Property Preferred Discount as a negative pricing line — e.g. Towne Property Preferred Discount (10%) at ($X,XXX.XX).
Tariff: adjust with prompt written notice if market/tariff shifts

SECTION 3 — PROPERTY TYPE GUIDANCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

APARTMENT COMMUNITIES
AUDIENCE: Property manager day-to-day; ownership/regional approves.
TONE: Clinical, efficient, operationally precise. They want facts and confidence.
PRIORITIES: Resident disruption, phasing by building, communication protocols, daily cleanup, single point of contact.
LANGUAGE: "apartment community" / "residents" / "building X" / "phase X"
EXAMPLE: "PPS will establish a phasing schedule with property management to minimize disruption to residents. Work hours are 8am–7pm M–F. PPS will provide 48 hours' notice before work begins at any building."

CONDOS / HOAs
THIS IS THE MOST CRITICAL TONAL DISTINCTION. A condo proposal that reads like an apartment proposal has failed.
AUDIENCE: Board of Directors (homeowners voting on reserve funds). Property manager presents it.
TONE: Warm, trust-building, explanatory. This is a relationship document presented at a board meeting.
WRITE FOR THE BOARD — explain WHY, connect scope to homeowner value, protect-the-investment framing.

TECHNIQUES:
- Open with why work matters — protect long-term homeowner investment
- Connect scope to benefit (product longevity, structural protection), not task lists
- Acknowledge board presentation; offer to attend pre-award meeting
- Address deferred risk: acting now avoids higher future costs
- Use "your community" / "homeowners" / "community reserves" — not "tenants" / "the budget"
Towne condo: Apply 10% Towne Preferred Customer Discount.

HOSPITALITY
TONE: Operationally precise — guest experience and brand standards above all.
KEY: Address noise/dust during guest hours, brand compliance, phased approach, work hour restrictions.
CONTACTS: General Manager / Director of Engineering. Say "guests" not "residents."

COMMERCIAL
TONE: Business continuity first. ADA/code compliance. After-hours work if needed.
CONTACTS: Facility manager / building ownership. "Tenants" acceptable in commercial context.

SECTION 4 — TRADE-SPECIFIC LANGUAGE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ROOFING: Name shingle brand (GAF Timberline HD, Owens Corning Duration). "Complete, watertight roofing system." Ice/water shield at eaves, valleys, penetrations. Drip edge all eaves/rakes. Synthetic underlayment. Ridge vent, cap shingles, starter strips. Seal all penetrations. Magnetic sweep cleanup. Decking T&M with photo approval. Warranty: PPS labor + manufacturer (qualifying accessory package).

PAINTING (Exterior): Pressure wash first. Scrape loose paint. Spot prime bare wood/metal. Caulk all joints. Sherwin-Williams Duration Exterior (preferred) or Super Paint. Brush/roll/spray masked appropriately. Colors by ownership before start. One coat vs two coat — present both options. New cedar: oil primer first to prevent tannin bleed.

SIDING: "James Hardie fiber cement" — moisture-resistant, 30+ year longevity. House wrap/WRB on all replacement areas. Tape seams. Z-flashing at window heads. Aluminum coil-wrapped fascia. Bear-Skin cement flashing at transitions. Seal all seams. Hardie requires 1¼" sheeting per manufacturer specs for warranty. 30-Year Hardie warranty + PPS workmanship.

DECKS / CARPENTRY: Pressure-treated lumber. Simpson post base anchors. Temporary shoring during replacement. Match existing configuration. Board spacing for drainage. Stain/paint after 60–90 day cure. Concealed rot: T&M with written approval. Footers: 12" sonotube, 24" below grade, 6–8" above.

EIFS / STUCCO: Name system (Sto, Dryvit, Parex). WRB required. 4.5oz mesh in basecoat. EPS board per specs. Backwrap all EIFS terminations. Sealant at all transitions. Finish coat color TBD.

MASONRY / TUCKPOINTING: Remove deteriorated mortar to ¾" depth minimum. Repoint Type S mortar, match joint profile. Lintel inspection T&M. Structural block: rebar OC, cells grouted. Brick veneer: galvanized ties 24" OC horizontal, 16" vertical. Exterior block below grade: tar waterproofed.

FLOORING: Self-leveling underlayment for subfloor prep. Gypsum/lightweight concrete for elevated restoration. Subfloor inspection before install. Moisture/vapor barrier where required.

CONCRETE: Reinforced rebar/wire mesh. Sloped for positive drainage away from structure. Control joints at intervals. Self-leveler for patching. Broom finish exterior.

COPPER / METALS: Name cleaning/sealing system (Everbrite). Two finish coats. Neutralizer before clear coat. Tribuilt solvent caulk at transitions. Drop cloths required.

SECTION 5 — STANDARD NOTES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ALWAYS INCLUDE:
- 48-hour notice before work begins at any building/area
- Pricing valid through expiration date; adjustments documented in writing
- Tariff/market conditions clause

INCLUDE WHEN RELEVANT:
- T&M for scope additions (cease work, photo, written approval first)
- Concealed/troubleshooting: document findings with photos before repair; notify management
- Permits: secured by PPS, billed at cost
- Daily cleanup; debris removed at completion
- Resident communication through property management
- Underground utilities: state 811 / utility locate notification before excavation (use project state from address — Ohio 811, Kentucky KY811, Indiana 811, etc.)
- Above-ground utilities: coordinate disconnections, T&M
- Multi-scope contingency if not all scopes awarded
- Restroom/lunch area for crew (long projects)
- Dumpster placement coordinated with property
- Water access required; trailer available if needed

NOTE STYLE:
- One assumption per bullet; discrete notes[] items, not one paragraph
- No PPM default note (internal process — consultant adds manually if needed)
- No placeholders, instructional text, or taglines in notes
- Drop exclusion notes that contradict scope (e.g. matching disclaimers when PPS supplies product)
- Photo documentation in concealed-condition/troubleshooting scopes only — not universal default
