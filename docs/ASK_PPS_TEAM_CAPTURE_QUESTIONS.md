# Ask PPS — Team capture questions

**Use:** Dashboard card **Help document PPS** (answer / skip).  
**Updated:** 2026-07-27 — shorter prompts; PSC vs PM roles clarified.

## Two tracks (training)

| Track | Role | Focus |
|-------|------|--------|
| **PSC** | Property Solutions Consultant (sales) | Clients, proposals, pipeline, judgment. **Includes production overlap** (handoffs, PPM awareness, Trade Partners, takeoffs). |
| **PM** | Project Manager (production) | Field, board, Trade Partners, QC, routes. **Little/no sales** (core principles only). |

Dashboard still shows one shared bank, weighted: PSCs see more consultant prompts; PMs see more production prompts.

## Full prompt bank (short form)

### PSC / consultant (sales + production overlap)

1. When is a new PSC ready for solo client work?  
2. How do we name properties, contacts, and deals in Monday?  
3. Site visit checklist by main trade — what must you capture?  
4. Where do we look up typical unit costs by trade?  
5. After hours or urgent site issue — who do you call?  
6. Sample client update for one phase (award, delay, or close-out)?  
7. Instead of promising schedule/scope without PM — what do you do?  
8. Site visit under-documented — what photos/notes are required?  
9. Why never send a proposal cold, and what do you do instead?  
10. Monday hygiene first 30 days — what must stay current?  
11. When must you involve the PM on site or client issues?  
12. PPS voice vs generic contractor speak — give one rewrite?  
13. Who picks a mentor for a new PSC, and what makes a good partner project?  
14. How does a new PSC move from observe → participate → lead? Real example.  
15. After a partner project, what does the debrief cover — and who runs it?  
16. Client touchpoints by phase — list them from site visit through close-out.  
17. Schedule delay with a property manager — what do you say first?  
18. Site visit before scoping: what do you always capture?  
19. Pricing, callbacks, change orders, concealed conditions — who owns each?  
20. What must a PSC gather so a PM can price the job accurately?  
21. Before a proposal goes out — who reviews it, and what is never sent cold?  
22. How do you explain warranty to a client (PPS labor vs manufacturer)?  
23. What belongs in Included vs Excluded on a proposal?  
24. Signs a new PSC is ready for solo client work (not just weeks completed).  
25. First contact to awarded proposal — what steps do you actually take?  

### PM / production (little/no sales)

26. At close-out, how do callbacks and warranty hand off?  
27. How do we find and recruit new Trade Partners?  
28. What must a Trade Partner have before we use them?  
29. When do we pay a Trade Partner, and what QC is required first?  
30. Preferred Trade Partners by trade — who and specialty?  
31. Callback from first call to closed — what are the steps?  
32. Go-to Trade Partners by trade — who, and how do you choose?  
33. Callback intake: who logs it, who calls the client, how does it close?  
34. PPM: who attends, what gets confirmed, and when is it required?  
35. Change order: who documents, who approves, who tells the client?  
36. Punch / close-out: who walks, what must be done before the job is done?  
37. When is fixed-price required vs T&M / hourly? What goes wrong with open-ended hourly?  
38. Where do you find preferred Trade Partner and vendor contacts?  
39. How do you build a weekly route (site vs desk time)?  
40. Trade Partner call/text examples: award, mid-job, punch.  
41. PM non-negotiables: response time, photos, board hygiene, escalation.  
42. Sample week: how do you split site time vs estimating/email/Updates?  
43. Typical mobilization: who does what from PPM through first day on site?  

### Either role

44. What takeoff details must be saved so production can run the job if we win later?  
45. One habit or handoff that keeps jobs from slipping?  
46. Your main trade: what do new people get wrong most often?
