# PSC & PM Training — Redesign from Field Corpus

**Source corpus (scrubbed 2026-07-27):**  
~1,290 files in iCloud `Proposals/`, ~74 client folders in `Projects/`, GS&R process docs (Proposal, Take Off, Pride Walk twin, Increase Margin, GSR), Hub voice guide, YIR/production lessons already in GS&R.

**Through-line (not a slogan to force on every slide):**  
**The Pure Way — Trust · Quality · Results**  
Commercial multifamily / cap-ex standard: professional, durable, clean execution — not luxury residential finish. Behavior over branding.

**Stance:** Current Hub PSC training (Rachel-informed modules + trade YouTube weeks) is a solid *skeleton*. It is light on *how PPS actually sells and scopes work* as proven by a decade of proposals. This doc is the blueprint to make both **PSC** and **PM** tracks deep enough to matter.

### 1.3 Proposal DNA (what “good” looks like on paper)

From scores of proposals + process docs + voice guide:

1. **Clear ownership of labor/materials** — labor only / labor+materials / noted materials  
2. **Active voice, PPS owns the work** — “PPS will…” not passive  
3. **Include / Exclude** — production’s bible when selling to Trade Partners  
4. **Address, not just property name** — production may never have walked it  
5. **Photos / markup** — include vs exclude visually  
6. **Special notes that protect trust** — 48-hour notice, access, dumpsters, staging, haul-off  
7. **Concealed / unit-rate T&M** — decking per sheet, drywall over fist size, mold T&M, manager approval  
8. **Options & phases** — building types, one coat vs two, silicone vs tear-off, flexible-season discount  
9. **Opinion with expertise** — “we recommend free-standing deck for cost and resident convenience”  
10. **Warranty discipline** — 2-year workmanship default; no deck floor coatings without approval; manufacturer systems called out  
11. **Updates = U / U2** — living documents  
12. **Commercial quality bar** — daily cleanup, leave cleaner than found, meticulous final inspection language (roof packages especially)

**Gap today:** Hub teaches Proposal Generator + voice drills. It does **not** drill the full **field proposal craft** that your process doc and real PDFs require.

### 1.4 Site / takeoff DNA

From Take Off / Pride Walk twin docs:

- Prep before arrival (maps, Google, tools, scope clarity)  
- Check in at office  
- Building types vs false twins (elevation changes)  
- Section measurements (front/rear/sides) — not one blob  
- LF vs SF; record raw numbers, math later  
- Measure for **ordering** when materials matter (board width, J-channel)  
- One good trip > three sloppy trips  

**Pure Way mapping:** Thorough takeoff = **Trust** (you don’t re-ask for access three times) + **Quality** (right materials ordered) + **Results** (job runs without rework).

### 1.5 Margin / production DNA (Increase Margin + Trey YIR)

- Clarify scope **in writing** before bid  
- Research trades (PPS, supplier, Trade Partner, web)  
- After award: shop suppliers; shop Trade Partners with confidence  
- Shift fair liability to Trade Partners in the sell  
- Visit sites; communication prevents free concessions  
- Save takeoff quantities for production (Trey 2024)  
- Fixed price vs open hourly traps  

**PM training** owns most of this; **PSC training** must teach enough that sales doesn’t set production up to fail.

## 2. Pure Way as a through-line (how to teach it)

Do **not** make every activity “write Trust Quality Results.”  
Do make every major skill answer: *Does this behavior increase trust, raise quality, and produce results?*

| Pillar | What it means at PPS (commercial MF) | Trainable behaviors |
|--------|--------------------------------------|---------------------|
| **Trust** | Client believes you’ll do what you said; production believes your scope; Trade Partners believe the sell | Check-in at office; 48-hr notice; no dates you can’t keep; review proposals live; include/exclude honesty; stop work + photo + written approval on extras |
| **Quality** | Durable, clean, code-aware commercial work — not custom home millwork | Prep before paint; decking inspection; product systems (GAF, SW); daily cleanup; punch before invoice |
| **Results** | Jobs finish, get paid, lead to next award | Complete packages; multi-scope logic; options that close; follow-up; bread-crumb → loaf strategy |

**Footer brand only** (voice guide): *Trust. Quality. Results.™* — not a Project Notes bullet.

## 4. Recommended architecture

### 4.1 Two tracks (keep clear)

| Track | Audience | Core | Overlap |
|-------|----------|------|---------|
| **PSC** | Property Solutions Consultant (sales) | Relationships, evaluation, proposals, pipeline, client communication | Enough production literacy to hand off clean work |
| **PM** | Project Manager (production) | Trade Partners, board, QC, schedule, materials, close-out | Pure Way + client presence; **little/no sales process** |

### 4.2 PSC program shape (12–14 weeks, denser middle)

**Block A — Identity & Pure Way in the field (Weeks 0–1)**  
- Mission + Pure Way behavioral map (table above)  
- Who we serve (archetype clients from Projects folders)  
- Stakeholder map: residents vs board vs ownership vs site staff  
- What “commercial quality” is (and is not)  
- Non-negotiables: no over-promise, punch before invoice, under-promise / over-deliver  

**Block B — How a PPS job is born (Weeks 1–3)**  
- Opportunity → site visit → takeoff → proposal → review conversation → award → PPM → live job → close-out  
- Partner projects 5–10 with observe → participate → lead  
- Monday + Hub as **system of record**, not the point of training  

**Block C — Craft of evaluation & proposals (Weeks 2–6)** — *heaviest rebuild*  
- Takeoff lab (your Take Off doc → graded exercises)  
- Proposal anatomy lab (include/exclude, special notes, options, U-updates)  
- Voice guide + rewrite of **real** proposals from vault (sanitize client names if needed)  
- Property-type writing: apartment clinical vs condo board  
- Unit rates & T&M patterns (decking/sheet, drywall size threshold, mold)  
- Photo markup standards  

**Block D — Trade depth, PPS-weighted (Weeks 3–9)**  
Reorder by book of business:

1. Exterior painting & wood  
2. Gutters / guards / cleaning  
3. Decks, rails, stairs  
4. Roofing (shingle + flat intro)  
5. Siding / Hardie / vinyl  
6. Concrete & exterior maintenance  
7. Interior paint / hallways / light renos  
8. Specialty / complex (permits, multi-trade) — Phil-style cases  

Each trade week: **problem diagnosis → measurements → include/exclude traps → quality standard → client communication → handoff notes** — not only YouTube install videos.

**Block E — Pipeline, accounts, Pure Way at scale (Weeks 8–12)**  
- Earn trust small → CapEx (Tony model; real small-scope examples)  
- Concentration risk / avg job size (Andy-type lessons)  
- Difficult conversations (delay, price, concealed conditions)  
- Callbacks & service recovery  
- Graduation: lead a full proposal + handoff a PM rates production-ready  

**Books:** Keep Infinite Game / Fanatical Prospecting / 4DX but **compress** so craft doesn’t lose weeks 9–12 entirely to reading.

### 4.3 PM program shape (parallel, production-first)

Seed exists in `pm_training_data.py` — expand to full track:

**Weeks 1–2:** Production Board, route, contacts, expectations  
**Weeks 3–5:** Trade Partner sell, QC, payments, fixed vs hourly (Trey cases)  
**Weeks 6–8:** PPM, mobilization, active job communication, materials  
**Weeks 9–10:** Change orders, callbacks, punch, close-out, margin protection  
**Weeks 11–12:** Multi-trade / complex, mentoring junior PMs  

Pure Way for PMs: **trust but verify**, quality on every walk, results = on-time/on-budget/happy site staff.

## 6. Sample Pure Way drills (ready to implement)

1. **Harbour Cove deck-style** — Recommend structural approach with cost + resident convenience; staining excluded; 48-hr notice. *Trust (honest exclude) · Quality (structure first) · Results (clear total).*  
2. **Wingate storage roof** — Unit price, decking T&M rate, schedule with office.  
3. **Falls at Landen concrete** — Small job done right: caution tape, match finish, haul-off, notice. Bread-crumb that earns next walk.  
4. **Arbors renovations** — Multi-unit package pricing, access, dumpsters, T&M for mold/drywall over threshold.  
5. **Hallway painting** — Include/exclude (floors, metal, unpainted), flexible season discount, corner-to-corner doors vs unit access.  
6. **False twin buildings** — Takeoff day: elevation change you almost missed.
