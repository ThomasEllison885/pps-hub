Privacy Policy

PPS Internal QuickBooks Tools
Pure Property Solutions / PPSOH LLC (“PPS”, “we”, “us”)
Effective date: August 3, 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies to the internal software tools and integrations operated by PPS that connect to QuickBooks Online (via the Intuit API) and related operational systems used by PPS staff (the “Tools”). These Tools are for PPS business use only and are not offered as a public consumer product.

2. Information we process

Depending on configuration and user authorization, the Tools may process:

  • QuickBooks company data you authorize (for example accounts receivable, invoices, customers, vendors, and related accounting records)
  • Authentication tokens needed to maintain a secure connection to QuickBooks
  • Basic operational logs (connection success/failure, timestamps) for troubleshooting and security

We do not sell personal information.

3. How we use information

  • To display financial and operational information to authorized PPS personnel
  • To support internal workflows (for example AR visibility, reporting drafts, and office operations)
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and debug connectivity issues
  • To comply with legal obligations

Use of QuickBooks data is limited to purposes consistent with the permissions granted during QuickBooks authorization and Intuit’s requirements.

4. Sharing

We may share information only:

  • With service providers that host or process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations
  • With Intuit/QuickBooks as required to operate the integration
  • When required by law, regulation, or legal process
  • With PPS owners, officers, or advisors as needed for business operations

We do not sell or rent QuickBooks data to third parties for marketing.

5. Storage and security

Credentials and tokens are stored with restricted access on systems controlled by PPS. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to an internal business tool. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Data retention

We retain connection tokens and cached extracts only as long as needed for operations, security, and recordkeeping, then delete or archive them according to internal policy.

7. Your choices

Authorized administrators may disconnect the QuickBooks integration at any time through QuickBooks app connections and by revoking tokens on PPS systems. Staff access is limited to people PPS authorizes.

8. Children

The Tools are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children’s data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made. Continued use of the Tools after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
